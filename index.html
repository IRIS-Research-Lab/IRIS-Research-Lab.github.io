IRIS Lab

Intelligent systems Robustness, Interpretability, and Security

The Intelligent systems Robustness, Interpretability, and Security (IRIS) Lab evaluates and advances the trustworthiness of AI and intelligent systems, specializing in perception systems. We uncover vulnerabilities in how these systems interpret visual information and use these insights to build systems that are more robust and reliable. Finally, we also leverage these vulnerabilities to develop tools for people to protect their privacy and autonomy as AI becomes increasingly ubiquitous. The IRIS Lab is directed by Prof. Blaine Hoak at the University of Texas at Dallas in the Computer Science department.
