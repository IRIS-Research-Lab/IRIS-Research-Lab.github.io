Join Us

We're looking for curious, motivated students who want to make AI systems more trustworthy.

Recruiting: The IRIS Lab is recruiting PhD students to start in Fall 2027.

Prospective PhD students

Apply to the UT Dallas Computer Science PhD program and mention Prof. Hoak in your application. If you're interested in our research, you're also welcome to email Blaine.Hoak@UTDallas.edu with:

  • The subject line [IRIS Lab] Prospective PhD student
  • A short introduction and your research interests
  • Your CV and any relevant experience (research, projects, or coursework)

Because of the volume of email, Prof. Hoak may not be able to reply to every message, but every one is read.

Current UT Dallas students

Master's and undergraduate students interested in research are welcome to reach out by email with your CV, transcript, and a few sentences about what you'd like to work on. Prior coursework in machine learning or security is helpful but not required.

Visitors and collaborators

We're always happy to hear from researchers interested in collaborating on robust, interpretable, and secure AI. Please get in touch by email.
